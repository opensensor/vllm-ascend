Exact activation-to-pack fusion
  Preserve the existing FP32 F.silu. A native kernel then widens the up operand,
  multiplies, applies the qualified saturation/NaN handling and FP16 rounding,
  and directly quantizes/packs the result. Low/high INT4 limbs, FP32 scales,
  and replicated quantized sums retain the existing packer's arithmetic.
  The intermediate FP16 tensor is no longer materialized in global memory.
  At 20480 routed rows its payload is 25 MiB; avoiding its write and subsequent
  read removes 50 MiB of logical tensor traffic, not measured PCIe traffic or
  an assertion about physical allocator release.

  The first 8-group kernel passed exactness and improved large-shape component
  time about 8%. Coalescing the strided input copies did not materially change
  that result. Increasing the tile to 16 groups improved it about 27%, and
  32 groups about 36%. The retained 32-group kernel uses 144 KiB of local
  workspace and preserves the eight-lane metadata layout. There is no new
  activation approximation. Earlier custom SwiGLU arithmetic was not reused.

  Complete activation plus packing, synchronized wall-time medians across ranks:
     2590 rows: 0.654..0.664 -> 0.478..0.480 ms
     5150 rows: 1.263..1.284 -> 0.817..0.822 ms
    20480 rows: 5.132..5.183 -> 3.268..3.289 ms
  The serving owner admits eager dense-ND contiguous FP16 [2560..20480,1280],
  FP32 compute and FP16 output. Other inputs, short batches, gradients and
  graph capture use the retained activation and grouped projection. No device
  value is read by the host in the normal path; descriptors remain owned.
  Launch failure retains its resources and prevents reuse. Shadow verification
