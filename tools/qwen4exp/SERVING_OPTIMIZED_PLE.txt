Installer correction
--------------------
The previous installer used a PLE-only resident switch. Such a switch restores
previous methods before applying its own patch set, which can remove retained
optimizations. The new installer uses the drained authenticated engine control
and attaches only at existing PLE eager and status boundaries. It preserves the
resident generation, candidate, native resources, weight storage, graph owners
and other worker control RPCs. Four common decode shapes are admitted first.
The worker retains the namespace, native managers and buffers for its lifetime,
including failed admission. A mismatch leaves the engine paused for inspection.
Actions off/on/verify toggle the existing owner without recapture or retirement.

  python -m tools.qwen4exp.apply_ple_serving \
    --run-root BASE/resident-live-v13 --action status --output NEW_RECEIPT
